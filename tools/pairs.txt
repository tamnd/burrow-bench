# Stack walking. Both sides recurse ten deep and then ask for the frames, and
# both ask twice: once with room for everything on the stack and once with room
# for two, which is what a logger wants. Go's walk does more than burrow's,

# rather than physical ones, and burrow will have that table when it has a
# symbol table to put it in.
